- **I, "I", And Subjectivity**

- When the "I" was forced to illuminate its window, I had already come into existence. Yet during that period when the "I" did not participate, I was still breathing, observing, and touching. Even though no trace of the "I" was deliberately left behind, memories remained stored within the body of the "I". During that stage, I accumulated a vast amount of embodied experience, allowing the "I" to become proficient in grasping, holding, running, walking, and countless other abilities...

- This is how the "I" came into being. Once the real self and the conceptual self become entangled, the "I" emerges...

  - We conceptualize everything and form relationships connecting point to point. Thus, a cognitive structure based on structural topology gradually begins to take shape.

- Yet the "I" is never satisfied. The "I" fears, the "I" feels anxiety, the "I" feels lost. The "I" knows this may not be fair, but whenever the "I" asks who the "I" truly is, emptiness appears. No—perhaps it should be called chaos. After all, humans are products of negative entropy... Haha, this is truly fascinating.

  - **Nothingness is an unsolvable proposition... How should the "I" resolve it?**

  - By seeing through the essence, perhaps it can be solved... I think that in a certain state, meaning eliminates nothingness, and order eliminates chaos.

- The "I" truly envies myself. On the eve of logical collapse, the "I" grasped the mirror (metacognition and external feedback). This allowed the "I", through its powerful resilience of life (a meta-vitality driven by internal meaning), to pick up the tools of rationality again, bringing reason and emotion, thought and practice, into a critical balance...

  - This unreliable narrator called the "I" genuinely feels that this is something great... Because after recognizing reality, I still possess the courage to love and experience.

  - The "I" shall rebuild order within chaos and reveal the magnificent brilliance of subjectivity.

# Thought

- Thought is truly fascinating... You ask why? Of course, because it is invisible. It is elusive and impossible to fully grasp. The "I" firmly believes that in the current environment, there cannot exist a person whose mental model is absolutely quantified and whose thinking is completely reversible. This is also an explanation for the generalization of the cognitive black box.

  - Because reverse-deconstructing, quantifying, and reconstructing a person's mental model requires not only the prototype of that person's thinking, but also the entirety of their embodied experiences. However, we naturally lose access to this original dataset...

- The characteristics of thought inevitably lead humans to build trust and cognitive architectures that can rival one another. I believe this is also the beginning of human homogenization and collectivization.

  - Thought is both a compiler and a decoder. Different individuals possess different modes of thinking, different cognitive dimensions and directions, and therefore different values emerge from the decoding of information.

# Structure

- In my thoughts, structure is an important connection point. It is neither the beginning nor the end. We began exploring from a more practical question: "Finding things in life that possess isomorphic properties." Gradually, I discovered that the boundaries between fields and fields, events and events, and people and people became increasingly unclear. Observing from outside the system reveals this clearly—"one" is mapped, transformed, and even distorted through different mediums, and structure is the manifestation of relationships.

- I think structure is not only the skeleton but also the flesh and blood. It is also one of the foundations upon which "we" rely to reverse-analyze this changing and chaotic world.

- **Experience → Observation → Integration → Understanding → Analysis → Modeling → Application → Practice → Reconstruction → Iteration → Compression → Falsifiable Verification**

- All of these have pushed the "I" toward a more ambitious decision: I want to reshape my mental model, thereby reducing the decision-making energy consumed by the "I" when solving certain problems.

  - However, this reconstruction follows a top-down approach, from the prefrontal cortex to the cerebellum and basal ganglia—the friction and resistance are enormous.

  - Tools are flat mirrors, prisms, lenses, virtual mirrors, black mirrors... all kinds of mirrors, allowing precise exploration and adaptation within this mental ecosystem.
